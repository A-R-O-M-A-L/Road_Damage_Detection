# 🚧 Cloud-Based Road Damage Reporting and Monitoring System

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Cloud-Based Road Damage Reporting and Monitoring System** is a web-based application designed to improve road safety and infrastructure maintenance by enabling citizens to report road damages such as potholes, cracks, broken surfaces, and waterlogged roads.

The system uses cloud technology to store, manage, and monitor road damage reports in a centralized platform. Users can submit reports along with photographs, location details, and descriptions of the damage. Authorities can review reported issues, track their status, prioritize repairs, and update progress until the damage is resolved.

The main objective of this project is to promote transparent communication between the public and road maintenance authorities while supporting faster, data-driven road maintenance decisions.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Enable citizens to report road damage easily.
* Capture road damage locations using GPS and map integration.
* Store reports, images, and related information securely in the cloud.
* Help authorities monitor, prioritize, and manage reported road issues.
* Track repair progress through status updates.
* Reduce the risk of accidents and vehicle damage caused by poor road conditions.
* Improve transparency, accountability, and efficiency in road maintenance.

## ✨ Key Features

* **User Registration and Login:** Secure access for citizens and administrators.
* **Road Damage Reporting:** Submit complaints with descriptions, images, and damage categories.
* **GPS-Based Location Tracking:** Identify the approximate location of damaged roads.
* **Interactive Map:** Visualize reported road damages on a map.
* **Cloud-Based Storage:** Store reports and images in a centralized cloud environment.
* **Admin Dashboard:** View, review, and manage reported road issues.
* **Damage Prioritization:** Classify issues according to severity and urgency.
* **Status Tracking:** Monitor reports through stages such as Pending, Under Review, In Progress, and Resolved.
* **Duplicate Report Management:** Help identify multiple reports concerning the same location.
* **Notifications:** Inform users about report updates and repair progress, when configured.
* **Analytics and Monitoring:** Generate summaries of reported, pending, and resolved road damages.
* **Report History:** Maintain records of reported problems and their resolution.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. A citizen registers or logs in to the application.
2. The citizen reports road damage by uploading a photograph and entering the required details.
3. The application captures or accepts the location of the damaged road.
4. The report and associated information are stored in the cloud.
5. Administrators review the report and assign a priority based on its severity.
6. Authorities update the report status as inspection and repair work progresses.
7. Citizens can track the report and check whether the issue has been resolved.
8. The dashboard displays road damage statistics to support monitoring and maintenance planning.

## ☁️ Role of Cloud Computing

Cloud computing provides the infrastructure for centralized data storage, remote access, and scalable report management. It allows authorized users and administrators to access road damage information from different locations without relying on a single local computer.

Cloud services can also support image storage, database synchronization, backup, and integration with mapping services.
